Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Video Copier is an internal tool operated by Imperium Management Agency ("we"). This policy explains how it handles data accessed through Google APIs.

What we access

With the agency's permission, Video Copier uses the Google Drive API to read, create, move, rename and delete files inside the agency's "Video Copier" folder in Google Drive. It only works with that folder.

How we use it

Videos placed in the folder are downloaded to our processing server, edited copies are created, and the copies are uploaded back to the same Drive folder. Temporary files on the server are deleted as soon as each video is finished.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine learning models. Video Copier's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Copies are kept in the agency's Drive and removed automatically after a set number of days. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
